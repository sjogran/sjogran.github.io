Family School Calendar

A personal tool a parent uses at home to help their child keep track of school deadlines.

Each week the child reviews a list of upcoming quizzes, tests, projects and due dates, and ticks the ones that are real. The ticked items are added to the child's own Google Calendar. Items the child un-ticks are removed, and nothing else on the calendar is ever changed.

The tool runs on one home computer for one family. It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.
